# General Instructions for Preparing the Case Study Report

## 1. Layout

-   Single-column layout.
-   Standard A4 page size.
-   Margins: 1 inch on all sides.

## 2. Spacing

-   Line spacing: 1.5 throughout.
-   Paragraph spacing: 6 pt before and 6 pt after.

## 3. Font Style & Size

-   **Font:** Times New Roman.
-   **Body text:** 12 pt.
-   **Headings:** Bold, 14 pt.
-   **Subheadings:** Bold, 12 pt.

## 4. Figures & Captions

-   All figures must be numbered sequentially (Figure 1, Figure 2,
    etc.).
-   Captions must be placed below the figure and centered.
-   Diagrams must be clear and original; screenshots should not be used.

## 5. Tables

-   Tables must be numbered sequentially (Table 1, Table 2, etc.).
-   Captions must be placed above the table.
-   Use consistent formatting. Gridlines are optional, but tables must
    be neat.

## 6. Equations

-   Equations must be typed and editable, not inserted as images.
-   Number equations sequentially (Eq. 1, Eq. 2, etc.).
-   Equation numbers should be aligned to the right.

## 7. References

-   All references must be cited in-text using the **(Author, Year)**
    format.
-   Follow **APA format** strictly.
-   Minimum **8 references**:
    -   5 recent references.
    -   3 foundational references.
-   Plagiarism must be below **7%**.
-   Proper paraphrasing and citation are required.
